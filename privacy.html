Vishwas App - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Vishwas ("the app") is a billing, stock and accounts app for jewellery shops, for Windows and Android. This page explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

Your shop data stays on your device

Bills, customers, suppliers, items, stock, vouchers and ledgers are saved in a database on your own computer or phone. We do not sell this data and we do not use it for advertising.

Information sent to our servers

  • Sign-up request: the name, shop name, mobile number, and optionally email, city and message you type when you ask for an account.
  • Account and subscription: your login (email and password, handled by our authentication service, Supabase) and the status of your subscription.
  • Shop profile and devices: your firm details and a device identifier, device type and last-seen time, so that sign-ins on your devices can be managed.
  • Sync between your devices: when you use the app on more than one device, your shop's records are synchronised through our server so that all your devices show the same data.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose "Sign in with Google" on the Backup screen, the app asks for one permission: access to the files it creates itself in your Google Drive (the drive.file scope). The app uses it only to:

  • create a folder named "Vishwas Backup" in your Drive;
  • upload your backup files to that folder, list them, download one when you restore, and delete the oldest ones to keep only the number you chose.

The app cannot see or change any other file in your Drive. Backup files go straight from your device to your own Google Drive. We do not receive or store them, and the developer cannot open them. You can sign out in the app at any time, and you can remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • We do not read your bills or customers for any purpose other than running the service you asked for.

Sharing

Information is shared only with the service providers needed to run the app (hosting and authentication), and where the law requires it.

Your choices

You can export or back up your data from the app at any time. To ask for your account and the data we hold about it to be deleted, write to us at the address below.

Security

Connections to our servers and to Google use HTTPS. Keep your password and your backups safe; anyone with your Google account or your device may reach your backups.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
