Vishwas App is a billing, stock and accounts app for jewellery shops, for Windows and Android. It is used by shop owners and their staff.

What it does

  • Make sale and purchase bills (GST and estimate), quotations and vouchers, and print them.
  • Keep customer, supplier and ledger accounts, including gold and silver balances.
  • Track items and gold / silver stock, old gold and rate cuts.
  • Reports, and backups of the shop's data on the device and, if the owner chooses, in the owner's own Google Drive.

Sign in with Google (optional)

The app asks to sign in with Google only for the backup feature. With the owner's permission it creates a folder named "Vishwas Backup" in the owner's own Google Drive and stores backup files there. It can see only the files it creates itself, never anything else in the Drive. Read the Privacy Policy for how Google user data is used, stored and deleted.
